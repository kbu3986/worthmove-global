1. The True Hourly Wage Formula

True Hourly Wage = (Annual After-Tax Net + Annual Perks/401k − Annual Commute Spend) ÷ (2,000 Base Work Hours + Annual Round-Trip Commute Hours + Annual Unpaid Overtime Hours)

We model 50 active working weeks per year (accounting for ~2 weeks of holidays/PTO), meaning a 5-day in-office schedule equals 250 commuting days/year. Hybrid WFH schedules proportionally reduce annual commuting hours.

2. Tax & Payroll Assumptions

  • United States: 2026 Single Standard Deduction ($15,000), progressive federal brackets (10%–37%), Social Security (6.2% up to wage base cap), Medicare (1.45% + 0.9% Additional Medicare), plus state-specific progressive or flat schedules.
  • UK, Canada & Australia: UK Personal Allowance taper + Income Tax/NI; Canadian Federal + Ontario + CPP/EI; Australian Stage 3 resident brackets + 2% Medicare levy.
  • Custom Override: Users with complex joint filings, pre-tax HSA/401(k) deductions, or other jurisdictions can select Custom Effective Tax Rate (%) for exact tuning.
