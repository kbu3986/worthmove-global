Frequently Asked Questions

How does WorthMove calculate True Hourly Wage?

WorthMove starts with your 2026 after-tax net pay (accounting for federal, FICA, and state/country progressive brackets), adds annual perks/401(k) match, subtracts annual transit/gas/parking expenses, and divides by your total life hours invested (2,000 base work hours + round-trip commute hours + unpaid overtime hours).

How much raise do I need to leave a remote job for a 5-day RTO offer?

A 60-minute one-way commute adds 500 unpaid hours per year (+25% life time) plus $3,600–$6,000 in after-tax transit or driving costs. Because raises are taxed at your marginal bracket, most workers need a 30%–40% gross salary bump just to break even on true hourly pay.

Does the PNG receipt or share link store my salary data on a server?

No. Everything runs 100% client-side in your browser. The PNG receipt renders locally on HTML5 Canvas, and Share Links encode your numbers directly in URL query parameters.
