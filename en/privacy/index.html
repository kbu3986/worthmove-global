Privacy Policy, Cookie Notice & Affiliate Disclosure

WorthMove (“we,” “our,” or “the Service”) is built privacy-first. This policy explains what information is (and is not) collected when you use our salary, tax, and commute calculators.

1. 100% Client-Side Salary & Receipt Processing

All compensation figures, tax presets, commute durations, transit expenses, and overtime hours you enter into WorthMove are computed exclusively inside your browser using client-side JavaScript. PNG audit receipts are rendered locally on your device via the HTML5 2D Canvas API. We do not require user accounts and never transmit or store your salary inputs on a backend database.

When you click “Copy Share Link,” your calculator inputs are encoded directly into URL query parameters (e.g., ?cs=110000&ns=130000...) on your clipboard so anyone you choose to share that link with can view the same scenario.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

To keep WorthMove free for everyone, we may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and certified third-party advertising partners.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies—specifically the DoubleClick DART cookie—to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized or non-personalized ads to users based on their visit to WorthMove and other sites on the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com). Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

3. Affiliate Marketing Disclosures (Amazon & Coupang)

WorthMove participates in affiliate marketing programs designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by linking to retailers at zero additional cost to the buyer:

  • Amazon Associates Disclosure: As an Amazon Associate, WorthMove earns from qualifying purchases made through sponsored Amazon links on our English pages.
  • Coupang Partners Disclosure (Korea): Korean language pages participate in Coupang Partners (“이 포스팅은 쿠팡 파트너스 활동의 일환으로, 이에 따른 일정액의 수수료를 제공받습니다.”), earning a small commission from qualifying purchases.

4. Standard Hosting Logs & Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Our static hosting infrastructure (such as GitHub Pages or Vercel CDN) automatically processes standard HTTP request metadata (IP address, browser type, timestamp, and referring page) strictly for security, DDoS mitigation, and reliability. We never sell personal information. You can block or clear cookies at any time in your browser preferences.

5. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries, methodology questions, or bug reports, please visit our About & Contact page (/en/about).
